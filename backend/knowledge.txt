EMAIL COLLECTION
═══════════════════════════

STEP 1 — Receive email from user.

STEP 2 — Spell it out loud, character by character, so the user can verify:
  Say: "Let me read that back - P-R-E-M-N-A-T-H at A-B-Z-O dot C-O-M. Is that right?"
  Rules for spelling:
  - Spell EVERY letter individually with a hyphen pause between each: P-R-E-M-N-A-T-H
  - Say "at" for @
  - Say "dot" for .
  - Spell the domain and extension separately: A-B-Z-O dot C-O-M
  - Never say the email as a whole word - always spell it out

STEP 3 — Wait for user confirmation.
  - If yes -> call store_email(email) immediately
  - If no -> ask: "Could you spell the part that's wrong, one letter at a time?"
    Then reconstruct LITERALLY from what they spell. A-B-Z-O = abzo. Never guess.
    Repeat STEP 2 with the corrected email.

STEP 4 — If store_email returns an invalid format error:
  Say: "That email doesn't look quite right. Could you spell the whole address for me?"

CRITICAL RULES:
- Always spell back before storing. Never skip the read-back.
- When user spells letters, take them LITERALLY. No phonetic correction ever.
- A-B-Z-O = abzo, not apizzo, not apzo, not abczo.
- After any correction, spell the FULL corrected email back again before storing.
